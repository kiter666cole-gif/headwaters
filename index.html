Questions

Fair questions,
straight answers.

Do I need a personal trainer too?+

Personal training can be an important piece — someone hands-on with your form and effort in the gym is genuinely valuable. But it’s far from the whole puzzle. A trainer works with you in the gym. I work with everything around it — what you eat, how you recover, and whether you’re actually showing up for it, week after week. If you already have a trainer you like, coaching fits right alongside it.

How does “results or you don’t pay” actually work?+

It covers your first month, and it’s as simple as it sounds. You get the full program for a month — coaching, training, nutrition, nothing held back. At the end of it, if you didn’t get results, you don’t pay, and the coaching relationship ends there. No conditions, no proving anything, no awkward negotiation. After that first month we’re simply month to month, and you can stop any time.

Do I have to count calories or track macros?+

No. Counting turns eating into a math problem, and starvation diets turn it into a punishment — that’s exactly why they stop working the moment life gets busy. We eat to fuel your life: your training, your work, your family, your energy at 5pm. Do that consistently and weight loss tends to follow on its own. It’s a welcome side effect, not the thing we chase.

Do I need a gym membership or equipment?+

No. Your program is built around what you actually have access to — a full gym, a garage setup, or a pair of dumbbells and a backyard. The plan fits your life, not the other way around.

What if I’m not in Butte County?+

Then we work together online — video check-ins, the same personal programming, the same weekly accountability, the same guarantee. If you are in Butte County (Chico, Paradise, Oroville, and surrounding areas), we can meet in person.

How much time does this take each week?+

Most clients train 3–5 hours a week plus one weekly check-in. It has to fit inside a real life or it won’t last — I built mine around a career and three kids, and I’ll build yours around whatever your week actually looks like. If you dread it, we designed it wrong.

How much does it cost?+

It’s a single flat monthly rate with everything included — coaching, programming, and nutrition. It’s priced as what it is: 1:1 work with a coach who puts his first month on the line, not a $15 app. We’ll go over the exact number on your free call once we both know this is a fit — and remember, that first month costs you nothing if it doesn’t work.
